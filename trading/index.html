Process

Four stages, every session.

The same sequence every time. Most of the work happens before the entry, and a good share of sessions end without one.

Plan

Levels, bias and the conditions for an entry are marked before the session opens, on screen.

Wait

No entry until those conditions line up. Sitting out is a decision, and we say so live.

Execute

Stop and position size are set before the order goes in. Targets are staged in advance.

Manage & review

Valid trades are left to play out. Every session is reviewed afterwards on the replay.

Live first

Why we trade on camera.

A screenshot after the fact proves nothing. A live stream shows the plan, the timing and the management as they happen, including the losing trades and the days we don't trade at all.

  • Entries are called before they happen, not explained afterwards
  • Every session is kept as a chaptered replay you can check
  • Results are published separately, with their source

Risk framework

Risk management comes first.

Strategy and psychology matter, but risk decides whether a trader lasts. Losses are part of trading and are handled by rule.

Defined before entry

The stop is set before the order goes in. Size is worked out from the stop distance, never the other way round.

Patience on both sides

Wait for setups that line up. Once a valid trade is on, let it play out calmly instead of interfering.
