Who can use the dashboard

You must be 18 or older and legally able to agree to these terms, and using FX GOAT's services and your broker's products must be lawful where you are. Your account is personal: don't share your sign-in with anyone.

Your account and sign-in

You sign in with a one-time code or link sent to your email address; there is no password to set. Anyone who can read your email can sign in, so keep your mailbox secure and tell support@fxgoat.com straight away if you think someone else has used your account.

Keep your details accurate. You can see changes to your account on the dashboard's Activity page.

Broker accounts and FX GOAT ALGO eligibility

  • You open and keep your trading account directly with the broker, under the broker's own terms and identity checks (KYC). FX GOAT never asks for your broker password.
  • FX GOAT ALGO access is currently free for trading accounts registered under FX GOAT's partner (Exness) or introducing-broker (Vantage) relationship. There is no minimum deposit or trading-activity requirement.
  • FX GOAT checks that relationship with the broker, automatically or through its team, and checks it again from time to time.
  • A broker or system outage, or a check that can't complete, is not treated as your account leaving the relationship. Access is withdrawn only after repeated checks confirm the account is no longer under the relationship, following a grace period.
  • If the same trading account is linked from more than one FX GOAT account, the team reviews the claims and may reject the one that doesn't belong to the account holder.
  • You can ask support@fxgoat.com for a person to review any check result.

Requests and TradingView access

Requests you send from the dashboard are reviewed by FX GOAT's team, which uses a private internal spreadsheet to process them. Sending a request does not by itself grant access.

FX GOAT ALGO is a TradingView indicator. Where access is approved, the team adds it manually to the TradingView username you give; it isn't granted automatically, and the timing depends on the team. Access can be removed if your eligibility ends or these terms are broken.
