Operating principles

How we build it.

Fail closed

If a check can't pass, the system stops. It does not send a partial or unverified message.

Human approval

Nothing publishes or goes live without explicit sign-off from the team.

Everything logged

Every decision is recorded, can be reviewed and can be rolled back.
